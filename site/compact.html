Inspect the models and data

Demand estimation

43,700 matched hourly observations across 2021–2025; 8,654 evaluation hours in 2025. Nonlinear temperature terms helped; a straight-line temperature term did not. This uses observed weather, not an operational demand forecast.

Price prediction

1,809 eligible daily forecasts across five years; 17 dates excluded. Operational HRRR issue/valid metadata and archive timing were checked before the cutoff. Realized future temperatures were not substituted.

Inspect the benchmarks and uncertainty

What followed—and what limits the result

The additional checks.

Did either follow-up improve the signal?

Forecast temperature trajectory

Selected rule: $1,121.88 gross versus $776.12 for matched linear, on 356 dates. The incremental confidence interval crosses zero. These are forecast changes—not realized temperature lags or delayed stock reactions.

Six-hour forecast revisions

Revision rule: $982.62 gross versus $1,020.96 for matched linear, on 362 dates. Comparing two forecasts for the same delivery hour slightly worsened performance.

Neither established reliable incremental alpha. Cohorts differ, so their raw totals cannot be ranked across tests. Both follow-ups are exploratory because 2024 and 2025 were already examined. Read the follow-up study ↗

Final models refit on 2021–2024. Preprocessing uses fitting rows only. Methods include OLS, piecewise-linear splines, calendar controls, matched benchmarks and dependence-aware block bootstrapping. 2025 had already been examined in preceding work; later stages are not fresh blind tests.

An initial price-history extension stopped because historical input versions could not be verified. The narrower test excluded those inputs. The project retains explicit data gates, source receipts, code hashes and negative results.

What should a reader not infer?

One zone, one weather point and one evaluation year limit generalization. Calendar-only predictive baselines do not establish value over strong price-history or commercial forecasts. No causal effect, production system, live trading or long-term climate projection is demonstrated.

Corrected archive prices do not recreate all historical training/invoice vintages. Forecast archive timestamps are availability evidence, not a full dissemination ledger. Exclusions can affect results; assumed clearing and omitted costs limit trading claims.
